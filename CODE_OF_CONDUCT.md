# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a friendly, harassment-free experience for everyone
participating in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions, and chat.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful in disagreement; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Gracefully accept and give constructive correction.
- Keep humour playful toward the code, never toward contributors.
- Stay on topic in project surfaces; take tangents elsewhere.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or insulting/derogatory comments.
- Public or private harassment of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Any conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional open-source setting.

## Scope

This standard applies in all project spaces — repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions, the website, and any official chat — and whenever an
individual represents the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
contributions that violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently
ban contributors for serious or repeated violations. Enforcement decisions are
made with the least intervention that restores a respectful space, and are
explained when it does not compromise anyone's privacy.

Report problems by opening an issue marked `conduct` or contacting the
maintainers through the Ding-Ding-Projects organization. All reports are
reviewed and handled with discretion.
